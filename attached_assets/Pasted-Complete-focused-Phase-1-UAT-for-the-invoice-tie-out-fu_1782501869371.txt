Tie-out formula to validate:
Expected Total = Subtotal + TaxAmount + FreightAmount + OtherChargesAmount - DiscountAmount

Tie-out statuses to validate:

* PASS: absolute difference is less than or equal to $0.01
* WARNING: absolute difference is greater than $0.01 and less than or equal to $0.05
* FAIL: absolute difference is greater than $0.05 when required values are available
* SKIPPED: subtotal or total is missing and tie-out cannot be calculated

Defect severity:
Critical:

* Material tie-out mismatch can be approved without warning or block
* Incorrect formula causes wrong expected total
* Discount is added instead of subtracted
* Other charges ignored when present
* Manual edits save to wrong invoice
* Validation data from one invoice appears on another invoice

High:

* Tie-out FAIL does not block approval
* WARNING incorrectly blocks approval
* SKIPPED becomes false PASS
* CSV omits required tie-out fields
* Review screen does not show tie-out explanation
* Audit log does not record manual amount edits

Medium:

* Explanation unclear
* Rounding difference displays incorrectly
* Minor formatting issue
* Status color/visual indicator confusing

Low:

* Cosmetic spacing or label issue
